MIL.ON.AIR Studio

MIL.ON.AIR is a Canadian discipline-wear brand voiced by the two hosts of the MIL.ON.AIR podcast, Renar D.Fox and Wolf Blackwoodz.

MIL.ON.AIR Studio is our internal publishing tool: it prepares our short videos (podcast moments and our Winter Arc collection) and, after a person on our team approves each one, publishes it to our own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts.
